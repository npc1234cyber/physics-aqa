1. Measurements and Their Errors
SI base units: mass (kg), length (m), time (s), amount of substance (mol), temperature (K), electric current (A). Candela excluded.

Prefixes: T, G, M, k, c, m, μ, n, p, f

Errors: Random vs systematic. Precision, repeatability, reproducibility, resolution, accuracy.

Uncertainty: Absolute, fractional, percentage. Combination by addition/subtraction (add absolutes), multiplication/division/powers (add percentages). Error bars on graphs. Uncertainties in gradient and intercept of straight-line graphs.

Estimation: Orders of magnitude; approximate values of physical quantities.

2. Particles and Radiation
2.1 Particles
Atom: proton, neutron, electron — charge and mass (SI and relative). Specific charge. ᴬ_Z X notation. Isotopes.

Strong nuclear force: short-range attraction (up to ~3 fm), very-short-range repulsion (<~0.5 fm). Keeps nucleus stable.

Decay: α decay, β⁻ decay (with neutrino). Conservation of energy → neutrino hypothesis.

Antiparticles: positron, antiproton, antineutron, antineutrino. Same mass, opposite charge. Rest energy in MeV.

Photons: E = hf = hc/λ. Annihilation and pair production (energies involved).

Four fundamental interactions: gravity, electromagnetic, weak nuclear, strong nuclear.

Exchange particles: virtual photons (EM force); W⁺ and W⁻ (weak interaction — β⁻, β⁺, electron capture, e⁻-p collisions). Feynman-style diagrams.

Classification:

Hadrons (feel strong force): Baryons (proton, neutron) and Mesons (pion, kaon)
Leptons: electron, muon, neutrinos (electron and muon types)
Baryon number conserved. Lepton number conserved (separately for electron and muon types).
Proton = only stable baryon. Pion = strong force exchange particle. Kaon decays to pions. Muon decays to electron.
Strange particles: produced via strong interaction, decay via weak. Strangeness conserved in strong interactions; can change by 0, ±1 in weak.

Quarks: up (u), down (d), strange (s) + antiquarks. Properties: charge, baryon number, strangeness. Combinations for proton, neutron, antiproton, antineutron, pions, kaons. Neutron decay. Quark character change in β decay.

Conservation laws: charge, baryon number, lepton number, strangeness. Energy and momentum conserved.

2.2 Electromagnetic Radiation & Quantum Phenomena
Photoelectric effect: threshold frequency, work function φ, stopping potential.

hf = φ + E_k(max)
Electron-atom collisions: ionisation, excitation. Electron volt (eV ↔ J conversion). Fluorescent tube.

Energy levels: discrete. Line spectra as evidence. hf = E₁ − E₂

Wave-particle duality: electron diffraction → wave properties. Photoelectric effect → particle nature.

de Broglie: λ = h/(mv)
3. Waves
3.1 Progressive and Stationary Waves
Progressive waves: amplitude, frequency, wavelength, speed, phase, phase difference.

f = 1/T, c = fλ
Longitudinal vs transverse. Polarisation as evidence for transverse. Polaroid, aerial alignment.

Stationary waves: nodes, antinodes. Two waves same frequency, opposite directions.

String first harmonic: f = (1/2l)√(T/μ)
3.2 Refraction, Diffraction, Interference
Interference: path difference, coherence. Young's double slit: w = λD/s. White light fringes. Laser safety.

Diffraction: single slit (qualitative — central maximum width varies with λ and slit width). Grating: d sin θ = nλ

Refraction: n = c/cₛ. Snell's law: n₁ sin θ₁ = n₂ sin θ₂. Total internal reflection: sin θ_c = n₂/n₁. Step-index fibre optics, cladding, material and modal dispersion, pulse broadening, absorption.

4. Mechanics and Materials
4.1 Force, Energy, Momentum
Vectors/scalars. Addition (calculation for right angles, scale drawing for others). Resolution into components. Equilibrium (2–3 coplanar forces).

Moments: force × perpendicular distance. Couple. Principle of moments. Centre of mass.

Straight-line motion: v = Δs/Δt, a = Δv/Δt. Graphs (displacement–time, velocity–time, acceleration–time): gradients and areas.

v = u + at, s = ½(u+v)t, s = ut + ½at², v² = u² + 2as. g.
Projectile motion: independent horizontal/vertical motion. Friction (qualitative). Lift, drag, terminal speed. Air resistance increases with speed.

Newton's laws: F = ma (constant mass). Free-body diagrams.

Momentum: p = mv. Conservation (1D). F = Δ(mv)/Δt. Impulse = FΔt = Δ(mv). Force–time graph area. Elastic/inelastic collisions, explosions.

Work/energy/power: W = Fs cos θ. P = ΔW/Δt = Fv. Force–displacement graph area. Efficiency = useful output power / input power.

Conservation of energy: ΔEₚ = mgΔh, Eₖ = ½mv². Applications with resistive forces.

4.2 Materials
Density: ρ = m/V

Hooke's law: F = kΔL. Elastic limit. Tensile stress, tensile strain, Young modulus = stress/strain = FL/(AΔL).

Elastic strain energy: ½FΔL = area under force–extension graph.

Plastic behaviour, fracture, brittle behaviour. Stress–strain curve interpretation.

5. Electricity
Basics: I = ΔQ/Δt, V = W/Q, R = V/I

I–V characteristics: ohmic conductor, semiconductor diode, filament lamp. Ohm's law as special case.

Resistivity: ρ = RA/L. Temperature effects on metals and NTC thermistors. Superconductivity (zero resistivity ≤ critical temperature).

Circuits: Series: R_T = R₁ + R₂ + ... Parallel: 1/R_T = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂ + ...

E = IVt, P = IV = I²R = V²/R. Conservation of charge and energy.
Potential divider: variable pd from supply. With variable resistors, thermistors, LDRs.

EMF and internal resistance: ε = E/Q, ε = I(R + r). Terminal pd.

6. Further Mechanics and Thermal Physics (A-level only)
6.1 Periodic Motion
Circular motion: ω = v/r = 2πf. Radian measure. a = v²/r = ω²r. F = mv²/r = mω²r.

SHM: a ∝ −x. a = −ω²x. x = A cos ωt. v = ±ω√(A² − x²). Max speed = ωA. Max acceleration = ω²A. Graphical x, v, a vs t.

SHM systems: Mass-spring: T = 2π√(m/k). Pendulum: T = 2π√(l/g). Energy variation (Eₖ, Eₚ, total) with displacement and time. Damping effects.

Forced vibrations/resonance: free vs forced vibrations. Resonance. Damping effects on sharpness of resonance.

6.2 Thermal Physics
Thermal energy: Internal energy = random kinetic + potential energies. First law (qualitative). Change of state: potential energies change, not kinetic.

Q = mcΔθ (including continuous flow). Q = ml.
Ideal gases: Gas laws (experimental). Absolute zero. pV = nRT = NkT. Work done = pΔV. Avogadro constant, R, k. Molar/molecular mass.

Kinetic theory: Brownian motion. pV = ⅓Nm(c_rms)². Derivation required (momentum conservation). For ideal gas: internal energy = kinetic only. ½m(c_rms)² = 3/2 kT = 3RT/(2Nₐ).

7. Fields and Their Consequences (A-level only)
7.1 Fields
Force field concept. Similarities/differences: gravitational (always attract) vs electrostatic (attract or repel). Both inverse-square.

7.2 Gravitational Fields
F = Gm₁m₂/r²
g = F/m = GM/r²
V = −GM/r. ΔW = mΔV. g = −ΔV/Δr. Equipotential surfaces.
Orbits: T² ∝ r³. Orbital energy. Escape velocity. Synchronous/geostationary orbits.
7.3 Electric Fields
F = Q₁Q₂/(4πε₀r²). ε₀.
E = F/Q. Uniform: E = V/d. Radial: E = Q/(4πε₀r²).
Trajectory of charged particle in uniform field.
V = Q/(4πε₀r). ΔW = QΔV. E = −ΔV/Δr. Equipotential surfaces.
7.4 Capacitance
C = Q/V. Parallel plate: C = Aε₀εᵣ/d. Dielectric action.
Energy: E = ½QV = ½CV² = ½Q²/C
Charge/discharge through R: Q = Q₀e^(−t/RC). Time constant RC. T½ = 0.69RC. Log-linear plotting.
7.5 Magnetic Fields
F = BIl (perpendicular). Fleming's LHR. Tesla.
Moving charges: F = BQv (perpendicular). Circular path. Cyclotron.
Flux: Φ = BA. Flux linkage: NΦ = BAN cos θ.
Faraday's/Lenz's laws: ε = NΔΦ/Δt. Rotating coil: ε = BANω sin ωt.
AC: I_rms = I₀/√2, V_rms = V₀/√2. Oscilloscope use.
Transformer: Nₛ/Nₚ = Vₛ/Vₚ. Efficiency = IₛVₛ/(IₚVₚ). Eddy currents, inefficiencies. HV transmission.
8. Nuclear Physics (A-level only)
Rutherford scattering: qualitative.

α, β, γ: properties, identification, absorption experiments. Applications (thickness measurement, medical). I = k/x² (inverse-square for γ). Background radiation.

Radioactive decay: random, constant decay probability. ΔN/Δt = −λN. N = N₀e^(−λt). A = λN. T½ = ln2/λ. Log graphs for half-life. Applications (waste storage, dating).

Nuclear instability: N vs Z graph. Decay modes: α, β⁺, β⁻, electron capture. Excited states, γ emission. Tc-99m.

Nuclear radius: closest approach (Coulomb equation). Electron diffraction. R = R₀A^(1/3). Constant nuclear density.

Mass and energy: E = mc². Atomic mass unit u. 1u = 931.5 MeV. Fission/fusion. Binding energy per nucleon graph.

Induced fission: thermal neutrons, chain reaction, critical mass. Moderator, control rods, coolant.

Safety: fuel handling, shielding, emergency shutdown, radioactive waste.

Option 9: Astrophysics
Telescopes: Refracting (two converging lenses, normal adjustment): M = fₒ/fₑ. Cassegrain reflector (parabolic primary, convex secondary). Reflector vs refractor merits. Radio telescopes. Rayleigh criterion: θ ≈ λ/D. Collecting power ∝ D². CCD vs eye.

Star classification: Apparent magnitude m (Hipparcos scale, mag 6 = dimmest visible, 1 mag = ×2.51 intensity). Absolute magnitude M: m − M = 5 log(d/10). Parsec, light year.

Black-body radiation: Wien's law: λ_max T = 2.9×10⁻³ mK. Stefan's law: P = σAT⁴. Inverse square law.

Spectral classes: O B A F G K M (temperatures, colours, absorption lines). H Balmer lines (n=2 state).

HR diagram: main sequence, dwarfs, giants. Axes: M (−10 to +15) vs T (50000–2500K). Solar evolution to white dwarf.

Supernovae/neutron stars/black holes: Type 1a as standard candles. Schwarzschild radius: Rₛ ≈ 2GM/c². Supermassive black holes.

Cosmology: Doppler: Δf/f = Δλ/λ = v/c (v≪c). Hubble's law: v = Hd. Age of universe. Big Bang evidence (CMB, H/He abundance). Quasars (active supermassive black holes). Exoplanet detection (radial velocity, transit method).

Option 10: Medical Physics
Eye: optical system, spectral response, rods/cones resolution. Lens: power = 1/f, 1/u + 1/v = 1/f, m = v/u. Myopia, hypermetropia, astigmatism corrections (dioptres).

Ear: structure, transmission. Intensity level = 10 log(I/I₀), I₀ = 1.0×10⁻¹² Wm⁻². dB/dBA scales. Equal loudness curves. Hearing defects.

ECG: principles, normal waveform shape.

Ultrasound: Z = ρc. Iᵣ/Iᵢ = [(Z₂−Z₁)/(Z₂+Z₁)]². Piezoelectric devices. A-scans, B-scans.

Endoscopy: fibre optics, TIR, coherent/non-coherent bundles.

MRI: proton precession, gradient coils, RF pulses, de-excitation signals, computer imaging.

X-rays: production (continuous + characteristic spectra), rotating anode tube. FTP detector. Contrast enhancement (barium meal). Attenuation: I = I₀e^(−μx), μₘ = μ/ρ, half-value thickness.

CT scanner: principles (narrow beam, detector array, computer processing).

Radionuclide: Tc-99m, I-131, In-111. Mo-Tc generator. PET scans. Gamma camera. Physical/biological/effective half-life: 1/T_E = 1/T_P + 1/T_B.

Option 11: Engineering Physics
Rotational dynamics: I = Σmr². Eₖ = ½Iω². Flywheels. ω₂ = ω₁ + αt, θ = ω₁t + ½αt², ω₂² = ω₁² + 2αθ. T = Fr = Iα. Angular momentum = Iω (conserved). TΔt = ΔIω. W = Tθ, P = Tω.

Thermodynamics: Q = ΔU + W. Isothermal (pV = const), adiabatic (pVᵞ = const), constant pressure (W = pΔV), constant volume. p–V diagrams (area = work). Four-stroke petrol/diesel cycles. Indicated/brake/friction power. Overall/thermal/mechanical efficiency. Second law: need source and sink. η = W/Q_H = (Q_H−Q_C)/Q_H. Max η = (T_H−T_C)/T_H.

Reversed heat engines: COP_ref = Q_C/W = T_C/(T_H−T_C). COP_hp = Q_H/W = T_H/(T_H−T_C).

Option 12: Turning Points in Physics
Electron discovery: cathode rays, thermionic emission (½mv² = eV), specific charge e/mₑ (Thomson). Millikan's oil drop: QV/d = mg, Stokes' law F = 6πηrv. Quantisation of charge.

Wave-particle duality: Newton's corpuscular vs Huygens' wave theory. Young's double slit significance. Maxwell: c = 1/√(μ₀ε₀). Hertz (radio waves), Fizeau (speed of light). UV catastrophe, Planck's quanta, Einstein's photoelectric explanation. de Broglie: λ = h/√(2meV). TEM and STM principles.

Special relativity: Michelson-Morley experiment. Two postulates (same laws in all inertial frames; invariant c). Time dilation: t = t₀/√(1−v²/c²). Muon decay evidence. Length contraction: l = l₀√(1−v²/c²). E = m₀c²/√(1−v²/c²). Bertozzi's experiment.

Option 13: Electronics
Devices: MOSFET (N-channel enhancement, V_DS, V_GS, I_DSS, V_th, switch/high input R). Zener diode (breakdown voltage, constant voltage source). Photodiode (photoconductive mode). Hall effect sensor.

Signals: analogue vs digital. Sampling, quantisation, sampling rate, bits/bytes, PCM. Noise recovery.

Op-amps (ideal): infinite open-loop gain, infinite input R. Comparator. Inverting: V_out/V_in = −R_f/R_in (virtual earth). Non-inverting: V_out/V_in = 1 + R_f/R_l. Summing amplifier. Difference amplifier. Real: frequency response, gain × bandwidth = constant.

LC filter: f₀ = 1/(2π√LC). Q = f₀/f_B.

Digital: AND, NAND, OR, NOR, NOT, EOR. Boolean algebra. Truth tables ↔ logic circuits. Counters (binary, BCD, Johnson, modulo-n). Astables (clock rate, duty cycle, mark-to-space, RC frequency control).

Communications: block diagram. Media: wire, fibre, EM (ground/sky/satellite). TDM. AM bandwidth = 2f_M. FM bandwidth = 2(Δf + f_M).
